Anthem

Health OS · Doc.ai

Overview

I worked on Health OS at Doc.ai, exploring how a shared interface could help patients, their families, and doctors make sense of a person’s health history.

The concept brought records, scans, appointments, and conversations into one timeline, so people could see what happened, understand the context, and discuss what comes next.

Approach

The design separated health events from conversations while keeping them connected. People could move from a timeline entry into the detail behind it—such as a scan or information about a treatment—without losing the larger picture.

The walkthrough includes interface designs and a filmed concept demo of a patient, family member, and doctor reviewing information together on a large screen.
