00CONCEPT

a ball that
pushes back.

orbi is a sealed sphere with no wheels, no legs and nothing sticking out. fourteen zipper-chain actuators sit inside it. each one pushes a round panel of the skin outward, and the sphere rolls away from the push. a low step is something to lever over.

01HOW IT MOVES

Azip

two toothed strands lie flat in the magazines either side of each drive block. a gearmotor turns a pair of sprockets that pull the strands together, their teeth lock, and they leave the top of the block as one rigid column that pushes a panel of the skin outward.

Bpush

a panel on the low trailing side extends until it meets the floor and pushes. the sphere rolls away on that momentum and coasts. when it slows, the next panel round takes a turn. to stop, the three lowest panels come down and hold it.

Cclimb

at a step the shell catches the edge. a trailing panel keeps extending, down to the lower floor, and levers the sphere round the corner and up onto the tread.

02FEATURES
14ACTUATORS

zipper-chain drive

six on the faces of a cube and eight on its corners. whichever way the sphere is facing, at least three panels can reach the ground.

1,900PIXELS

pixel skin

rgb leds sit under the gloss-white skin and across every panel. only the lit pixels show. a level line holds steady while the body rolls under it.

12PODS

sensor ring

range and sound pods sit under the skin between the panels, on the twelve edge directions. nothing shows on the outside, and there is no blind side.

14PADS

touch panels

each panel measures the force it pushes with. the robot feels the ground it stands on, and feels a hand.

360°HOLONOMIC

no front, no back

it never has to turn round. heading is a choice of which panel fires next.

0EXPOSED PARTS

sealed sphere

with every panel home the surface is a closed ball. nothing to snag, nothing to pinch.
