# MindOS

Spatial thinking · Virtual reality · TEDxSanFrancisco

## Overview

Why are big ideas so hard to share? After years of working together, engineer Serban Porumbescu and I kept running into the same problem: we could explain an idea, but the connections behind it were much harder to communicate.

MindOS grew out of that question. We built a virtual reality workspace where books, websites, videos, and 3D objects could become part of a connected map. A way to explore an idea and the thinking that led to it.

## Approach

I approached the experience through physical interactions: pick something up, give it a place, connect it to something else, and change its scale. The space around you becomes a canvas for thinking.

The prototype let us show how an idea could expand into its references and relationships, then collapse back into a larger picture. We brought that vision to the TEDxSanFrancisco stage in 2016 with a demo of an entrepreneur’s connected world.

## Thinking with your hands

These demos are silent. Use each video’s controls to play or pause it.

[Pick up and place a 3D reference in the MindOS workspace](https://www.brett.red/assets/mindos/pick-and-place.mp4)

### Give a thought a place

Take a reference from the collection and place it in the space around you. A 3D object becomes something you can position, return to, and build an idea around.

[Anchor a lightbulb representing an idea in virtual space](https://www.brett.red/assets/mindos/anchor.mp4)

### Anchor the idea

A lightbulb gives the central idea a visible home. Position it in the workspace, then arrange the thoughts and references that help explain it.

[Connect books, web pages, and objects with visible lines](https://www.brett.red/assets/mindos/connect.mp4)

### Make the connections visible

Draw relationships between pieces of information. Books, web pages, and objects become a map someone else can explore, with the context behind the idea still attached.

[Expand and collapse a connected model with two-handed gestures](https://www.brett.red/assets/mindos/scale.mp4)

### Change your perspective

Use both hands to change the scale of the model. Expand a connected world to explore its detail, then bring it back down to see how the pieces fit together.

## The future of sharing ideas

Serban and I shared the question behind MindOS, the limitations we saw in familiar tools, and a demonstration of the workspace at TEDxSanFrancisco. The ambition was to let someone explore the depth of an idea through its connections.
