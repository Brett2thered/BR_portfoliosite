Design engineering

Prototype the question, not just the screen.

Design engineering helps a team learn faster by putting the interaction itself in front of people—before a plan hardens into a build.

The practice

Use making to reduce ambiguity.

A prototype can answer questions that a deck cannot: Does the interaction feel natural? Is the feedback immediate enough? Does a new input model make sense in the hand, in motion, or in the user's actual environment?

For Brett, code and prototypes are part of the design toolkit. They make the conversation with product and engineering teams more concrete, expose constraints early, and create a shared artifact to test.

Where it matters

New inputsWearables, touchless interaction, and other interfaces that need to be experienced rather than merely described.
Spatial productsAR prototypes and map-based experiences where context, motion, and place shape the design.
Product systemsInteraction and visual systems that help a team build consistently while retaining product character.
