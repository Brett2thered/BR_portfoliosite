AI product design

Make the capability feel useful.

AI-first product design is not a prompt box added at the end. It is the work of making a powerful system understandable, steerable, and safe enough to trust.

The stance

Start with the human decision.

The important design question is not “where can AI go?” It is “what decision, task, or moment of uncertainty becomes meaningfully better with it?” Good AI experiences make the system's role clear, keep people in control, and let them inspect or revise consequential output.

That is the same product discipline that matters in every complex product: understand the user, reveal the right amount of complexity, and make the next action obvious.

Design principles

LegibilityExplain what the system can do, what it used, and where uncertainty remains.
ControlGive people useful choices, clear revision paths, and a way to recover from a bad result.
ContextPut assistance inside the workflow rather than asking people to translate their work into a generic chat interaction.
ProofMeasure whether the experience helps people complete the job, not merely whether they use the feature.
