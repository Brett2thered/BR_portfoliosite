## Design Context

### Users
Prospective clients, collaborators, and hiring teams exploring Brett Red’s product-design and design-engineering portfolio. They need to understand the work quickly and try working examples.

### Brand Personality
Hands-on, expressive, clear. Preserve the creator’s established portfolio rather than rebrand it.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use the existing masonry cards, consistent gutters and rounded corners, and white editorial case-study pages. For Medicare, the supplied hero screenshot is the visual source: teal typography, people-first photography, and a layered Medicare card. Retain the existing portfolio typography.

### Design Principles
- Show the work, then explain it plainly.
- Keep the established mobile project sequence and Thank You last.
- Give motion a clear purpose; support pause and reduced motion.
- Make prototypes genuinely usable, with clear sample-data labeling.
- Preserve performance and consistent card geometry at every breakpoint.
